## Textbooks
- **Main:** *Essentials of Economics* — Paul Krugman, Robin Wells, Kathryn Graddy (Worth, 2014). ISBN 0230019285
- **Other:** *Naked Economics* — Charles Wheelan (Norton, 2019). ISBN 9780393356496

## Course objectives (as written)
1. What is economics? 2. How consumers behave? 3. How producers make decisions? 4. What is the role of government in economy?
5. Why there are business cycles? 6. What is fiscal policy? 7. What is monetary policy? 8. How is exchange rate determined?
