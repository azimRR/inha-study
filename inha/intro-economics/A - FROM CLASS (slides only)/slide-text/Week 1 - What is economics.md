## Slide 32: An exampleof marginal decision

An example of marginal decision

Marginal Decision: A bit more or a bit less from the situation you
are now

▪Situation: Should I hire one more worker?
▪Marginal cost: The cost when I hire one more worker = w (wage)
▪Marginal benefit: The benefit I can get when I hire one more. (= increased product x price)
This increased product by this worker = MPL
I can sell this much at the market price (p) per unit.
* MPL is called as marginal product of labor.
So, if my marginal cost is less than my marginal benefit, i.e.

w < p * MPL, then I should hire one more worker
* P is already decided in the market from a price-taker’s perspectives
