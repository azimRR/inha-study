## ⚠️ When the lecture text and the slides disagree
The class review's rule: **learn the lecture-text (.docx) version first and the slide version second.** If a quiz offers only one of the two values, pick that one. One exception:

**Stars in Ulugh Beg's Zij.** The lecture text says **1118**, but the slide *and* the museum photo on it say **1018**, and outside sources confirm it (992 observed plus 27 copied from al-Sufi). The 1118 is almost certainly a typo. Review Q27 deliberately asks for the lecture-text value, so its key is 1118. **If a real quiz offers both, pick 1018.**
