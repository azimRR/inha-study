## Slide 2
General Rules
Attendance will be calculated in accordance with your participation in multiple-choice questions sessions (MCQS) which will be conducted every week. Please note that if you miss the MCQS more than 3 times, you will be considered as failed the course.  
The MCQS will consist of 5 questions, and they will be based on the recorded videos and textual materials, which are available at the University site (eclass.inha.ac.kr). You will have 10 minutes, and you can pass MCQS through eclass. MCQS is available from Monday to Friday this week.
After having every 3 lectures you will have to pass the quiz. The days of quizzes will be announced later.

## Slide 4
Coverage of the Subject
In the first half:
the history of formation of philosophical thoughts through long historical periods
the features of development and the state of art of the subject in taken period
the scientific heritage of Central Asian scholars on the development of sciences, in particular, social sciences.
In the second half:
the branches of philosophy such as ontology, epistemology and categories of philosophy
recent reforms on economy, the changes in social life and the restoration and development of spiritual heritage in Uzbekistan.
