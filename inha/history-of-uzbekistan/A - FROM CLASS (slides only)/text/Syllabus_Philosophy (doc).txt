Course Objectives (Less than 500 words)
1) To develop in the students' habits of clear, critical thinking within the framework of both historical and philosophical methodologies, as well as accepted norms of scholarship;
2) To develop in the students the skills of investigation, analysis, evaluation, and presentation;
3) To have some knowledge and understanding of the concepts of philosophy as well as philosophical schools and trends that emerged in different historical periods.
Course Description (Less than 500 words)
The subject will examine following issues:
- historical formation of the subject, main stages and trends in the development of philosophy; categories and laws of philosophy.
The branches of philosophy such as ontology, philosophy of knowledge, philosophy of values 
- types of knowledge. Levels of sensory, empirical, scientific, logical and intuitive knowledge;
- the main issue of social philosophy. Philosophical analysis of society. Philosophy of law. The philosophy of history.

Class Structure (Less than 500 words)
In the first half of the semester(1-7 weeks), students will be introduced the history of formation of philosophical thoughts through long historical periods, the features of development and the state of art of the subject in taken period, as well as, the scientific heritage of Central Asian scholars on the development of sciences, in particular, social sciences. In the second half of the semester (9-15 weeks), students will be familiarized with the branches of philosophy such as ontology, epistemology and categories of philosophy.  In addition, recent reforms on economy, the changes in social life and the restoration and development of spiritual heritage in Uzbekistan will be examined.
