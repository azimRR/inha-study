- **First half (weeks 1–7):** the formation of philosophical thought across historical periods, including the scholarly heritage of Central Asian thinkers. This is the midterm scope. On eclass these are **Themes 1–7** (Theme 8 is uploaded in week 8).
- **Second half (weeks 9–15):** branches of philosophy (ontology, epistemology, categories) and reforms in Uzbekistan.
- **Textbooks:** Fullerton, *An Introduction to Philosophy*; *The Philosophy Book: Big Ideas Simply Explained*; Hugo, *Thinking and Writing about Philosophy*; William J. E., *Introduction to Philosophy*.
- **Office hours:** Prof. Yakubov, B-412, Tue/Thu 14:00–15:00, k.yakubov@inha.uz.

## Themes on eclass
1. Introduction to Philosophy

3. Early Middle Ages philosophy in Central Asia
4. Philosophical thought in the Timurid period
5. Western philosophy in the Middle Ages
6. Philosophy in the modern and contemporary periods
7. Ontology
8. Philosophical laws, categories and methods
