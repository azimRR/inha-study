## Objectives (as written)
By the end of the course you should be able to:
- write messages in the **four C's**: clear, concise, coherent and complete;
- understand the fundamentals of business communications (proposals, reports, etc.);
- become a confident speaker and effective communicator;
- apply managerial-communication skills to collaborate with colleagues.

The course covers classic writing principles, storytelling techniques, business communiqués and public speaking, in four parts. "Writing is thinking on paper."
