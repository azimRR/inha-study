What is Plagiarism?

• Plagiarism is the use of someone else’s ideas or language
  without acknowledging that we did not create them.

• It applies to ideas, words and unusual structures regardless of
  where we nd them — in a book, on a webpage, in an email.

• When including person’s information or wording in a document,
  we must acknowledge the source and include a citation that will
  tell your readers where we obtained it

• Plagiarism is sometimes seen as intellectual theft — plagiarism.
