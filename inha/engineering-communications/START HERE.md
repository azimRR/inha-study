## How to study (writing course)
1. Read the class notes for each week, then do the **practice tasks**. Each has a model answer to compare against.
2. Every day, write: one paraphrase, one SPQ, one developed paragraph, three leads for the same facts, and one professional email.
3. Check your writing against the four C's and the class checklists (the inverted-pyramid checklist and the paragraph development rules).
